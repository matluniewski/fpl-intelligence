# Public-source research policy

Status: active policy

Last updated: 2026-08-26

## Purpose

This policy permits exploratory research on publicly accessible football, FPL, club, journalist, creator, and news sources. It does not grant any right to operate a production source, display source content, or commercially reuse collected material.

## Permitted research

Research may use automated collection where the applicable source terms, technical access method, and law permit it. Approved methods may include official APIs, RSS feeds, documented exports, and non-authenticated public web access.

Researchers may inspect source activity, identify relevant accounts, classify signal types, measure posting cadence, and assess potential product value.

## Collection limits

Collection must be proportionate to the stated objective. It must not:

- bypass authentication, paywalls, robots controls, rate limits, CAPTCHAs, access controls, or other technical protections;
- use private, leaked, purchased, or user-session data;
- collect credentials, cookies, direct messages, private-account material, or other non-public content;
- create a production data feed, public product feature, or commercial reuse right merely from research access;
- retain raw content longer than necessary for the research objective; or
- represent inferred, copied, or unverified information as official fact.

## Records and retention

Each research run must record the source, access method, collection time, purpose, observed signal category, and relevant access or reuse restriction. Store only the minimum data necessary for evaluation; prefer account identifiers, timestamps, URLs, aggregate observations, and structured annotations over copied source content.

## Promotion to product use

A source may be promoted from research to a runtime or product source only through a separate, documented source review. The review must establish the permitted access method, intended use, rights, retention and deletion rules, attribution or display requirements, rate and cost limits, provenance requirements, and an operational disablement path.

When source terms, rights, or the permitted access method are unclear, research may record the source as a candidate but must not collect content automatically or enable it in the product.
